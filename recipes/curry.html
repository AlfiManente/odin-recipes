Yellow Curry

Description

Fragrant, warm and spicy yellow curry. This is a vegan recipe for the dish, which can altered if meat is preferred. One of the most complete dishes a vegan can eat, full of healthy fats, protein, carbohydrates and most importantly taste!

Ingredients:

  • Yellow Curry Paste
  • Coconut Milk
  • Firm Tofu
  • Red or Yellow Lentils, as preferred
  • Onions, Garlic, Bell Pepper(, Pak Choi)
  • Side: White Rice, either Jasmin or Basmati

Steps:

  1. Cut vegetables
  2. Pre-cook rice
  3. Pre-cook lentils
  4. Take only the fatty part of the coconut milk and heat it up until the fat is broken, meaning until the translucent fat from the coconut milk gets seperated
  5. Add a good amount of curry paste (1/2 table spoons)
  6. Add in the vegetables and fry until they catch some color
  7. Add the tofu and fry
  8. Add the pre-cooked lentils and the liquid from the coconut milk
  9. Let simmer until tastes combine
  10. Serve over white rice
