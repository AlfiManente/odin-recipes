Berry Porridge

Description

Quick, healthy and tasty breakfast. Full of fiber and vitamins to start your day the right way. Sweet and delicious.

Ingredients:

  • Oatmeal
  • Plant milk (I recommend soy milk)
  • Berries: Strawberries, Blueberries, Raspberries, Redcurrant
  • Soy Crispies and Soy Flakes (for added protein)

Steps:

  1. Take your oatmeal into a hot pan and roast it for a nutty taste
  2. Add the berries and let them cook for a bit
  3. Add the milk and let everything combine
  4. Let simmer until the porridge thickens
  5. Serve warm in a bowl, add more berries and soy crispies and soy flakes as topping
