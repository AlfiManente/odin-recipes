Pasta all'arrabbiata

Description

Spicy mediterranean classic. A bowl of starchy goodness, with a hot tomato sauce. Very easy and simple to make, but heartwarming and filling nonetheless.

Ingredients:

  • Favorite Pasta (Penne is recommended)
  • Tomato Passata
  • Garlic
  • Spicy Pepper
  • Native Olive Oil
  • Topping: Grated Parmesan Cheese

Steps:

  1. Cut Garlic and Pepper into thin slices
  2. Take a pan and heat up a good amount of olive oil
  3. Fry Garlic and Pepper until golden
  4. Add the Tomato Passata and let it reduce until the olive oil sets on top of the sauce
  5. Cook your Pasta until al dente
  6. Marry the Pasta with the sauce
  7. Serve with grated parmesan on top
